    MONITOR AT YOUR PACE

    Make it your own.

    Pause monitoring before changing your schedule or keywords.

    01

    Refresh schedule

    Each keyword’s timer starts after its scan finishes. If the queue is busy, overdue keywords show their wait time and run when a slot opens.

    More simultaneous pages increases site load and may trigger verification or restrictions. Start with one. No setting bypasses the site’s protections.
    02

    Search scope and filters

    Each keyword is refreshed in place in a single tab. Pages per search controls how deep the reviewer portal is read before the next keyword starts; one page is the gentlest setting.

    Filters apply to the products shown in this dashboard. Items whose value is unknown are listed separately instead of being guessed. Deeper pages mean more requests to Walmart — use one page unless you need more. The extra-item markers are the characters that are tried at the end of a keyword when a keyword has the ⚗ switch on.
    03

    Telegram to your Android

    Optional. Create a bot with @BotFather, send /start to your bot, then enter its token and your chat ID. Tokens remain on this computer and are never returned to the dashboard.

    04

    Notifications

    New products are announced with the product title and its value. A system notification (Windows toast) is raised by the companion, so it arrives even with this dashboard closed. Your PC and the companion must stay running.

    A system notification looks like this: New product · chair — Massage Chair Cushion Pad — Value: $1,299.95. Removals are never announced.

    No Telegram token saved
    NO EXTENSION. NO CONSOLE PASTING.

    Your browser, connected.

    Run the companion on your computer. Sign in once in its dedicated browser.

    01

    Run the local app

    On Windows: download this folder, double-click setup.bat once (it installs Node.js and the packages for you), then double-click start.bat every time you want to use it.

    Anywhere else: install Node.js 22+ and Chrome, open a terminal inside SRC, and run:

    npm ci
npm start

    Open http://localhost:8080. This hosted preview cannot control your local browser.

    02

    Sign in to Walmart

    Click Open browser. A visible Chrome window opens with a dedicated profile. Sign in to Walmart and open the reviewer claim-product page. This does not attach to or copy your regular browser profile.

    Your login stays in that local profile. No password is entered into this dashboard.

    03

    Save your keyword list

    The keywords from your screenshot, plus power bank, are prefilled. Edit, add, remove, import, or export your list. Include the unfiltered highest-value page if you want to monitor it too.

    All links use /reviews/claim-product—never the retail search bar. Sort order, pages per search, and value range live in Preferences.

    04

    Start, then stay in control

    Click Start. Each keyword is read in a tab that stays open and is refreshed on every cycle, so the browser keeps your session warm. The first scan creates a silent baseline.

    Verification or sign-in requests pause the whole queue and leave the affected tab open. Resolve them manually, then Resume. We do not bypass anti-bot checks; Walmart may still restrict automated access.

    05

    Use the Chrome you already run

    Prefer your everyday signed-in Chrome? Close Product Pulse, then start Chrome with a remote debugging port and a throwaway profile folder:

    chrome --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir=%TEMP%\pulse-chrome

    Run npm start again and click Attach to my Chrome. The monitor drives that window instead of opening a second one. Still no extension.

    06

    Jump straight to a claim

    Every card has Claim on Walmart. It opens the reviewer portal in your browser, scrolls to that exact item, and highlights it — you still click Walmart’s own Claim button.

    Nothing is ever claimed automatically, and no anti-bot protection is bypassed.

    07

    Which browser?

    Product Pulse drives a real browser so it can use your Walmart sign-in. Pick the one you want it to open — you can change this later.

    i

    “View item” is the entry action shown in your screenshots. We include cards with an enabled View item / Claim action and exclude Out of stock / Shop similar cards. We never click a claim action. A later claim is not guaranteed; eligibility can change. Layout matching needs a first live scan on your PC.
